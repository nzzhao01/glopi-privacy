Glopi – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Glopi is an experimental WhatsApp bot built for a hackathon. This page explains what happens to the information you send it.

What we receive

When you message Glopi on WhatsApp, the WhatsApp Business Platform (operated by Meta) forwards the message to our service. This includes your WhatsApp phone number, your WhatsApp profile name, the message content and its timestamp.

How we use it

Messages are used only to test and run the Glopi bot. They are not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Storage and retention

Glopi does not store messages in a database. Messages may appear temporarily in the developer's local application logs during testing and are deleted when testing ends.

Third parties

Messages are transmitted through WhatsApp and Meta, whose own privacy policies apply: WhatsApp Privacy Policy.

Your choices

You can stop sending messages to Glopi at any time. To ask for any data about you to be deleted, open an issue at github.com/nzzhao01/glopi-privacy/issues (do not include personal information in the issue).
